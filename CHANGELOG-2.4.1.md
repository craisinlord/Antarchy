# Antarchy 2.4.1

- New biome, The Sky Isles. Floating islands with secrets to discover, and craters below them
- Elythia no longer has deepslate, and Rockite now changes color based on elevation
- The top layer of clouds is now puffy like the rest
- Glimmer Sigils are made with Rockite now
- Firefly particles spawn more abundantly and closer to the grass
- Primordial grass now has 3 height variants and can be seen from much further away
- River water now flows much smoother over height changes and turns
- Reduced number of giant lily pad spawns
